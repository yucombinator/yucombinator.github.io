Calgary in Motion

Every scheduled trip across Calgary — the Red and Blue CTrain lines, the MAX bus rapid transit lines and the Calgary Transit bus network — drawn as arrows moving along their routes, plus the regional services around them: Airdrie Transit, Cochrane's COLT, Falcon Bus on the Cochrane and Okotoks commuter corridors, and Roam Transit in Banff and Canmore. Chestermere's link is already in the Calgary feed: MAX Purple runs out to East Hills and across the lake. Switch between the latest weekday, Saturday and Sunday, and click any arrow or line for that route's stops and stats.

This is mostly the timetable. Each arrow is interpolated along its GTFS shape between its first and last stop time; delays and cancellations are not included. The bar chart counts scheduled departures per 10 minutes for the modes you enable.

The regional layer is an approximation. Calgary Transit is the only operator in the region that publishes GTFS; Airdrie, Cochrane, Falcon and Roam publish PDF timetables only. Those routes are rebuilt here from their published schedules and their own route GIS where the operator publishes one (Airdrie and Cochrane), so headways, spans and alignments are close but not exact — treat them as an illustration of the service, not a trip planner. Falcon's alignments are corridor approximations between its published stops, and Roam's alignments come from the last feed it published (July 2024) with times taken from its 2026 fall/winter timetables. Roam's seasonal routes — 4 Cave & Basin, 6 Lake Minnewanka, 9 Johnston Canyon and 11 Lake Louise Local — are not drawn because they are not running this season.

On-It Regional Transit, which used to run the Cochrane and Okotoks commuters, ceased service in June 2026; Falcon Bus took over both corridors, so that is what is drawn here. Strathmore and High River have no fixed-route transit, and Okotoks' own local loop publishes no geometry — none of those appear on the map.

When the Live button is available, real vehicle positions from Calgary Transit's GTFS-realtime feed replace the scheduled arrows for its routes. The button only appears when a snapshot less than 30 minutes old is loaded.

The Future toggle draws what is being built next: the Green Line, whose Phase 1 runs from Shepard in the southeast to a street-level terminus at 10 Avenue and 2 Street SW, in construction since June 2025, and the planned northern Phase 2 up Centre Street. Their alignments come from the City's Green Line Alignment dataset; the downtown portion follows the surface route Council approved in September 2026, which replaced the earlier tunnel plan, so treat the central few hundred metres as indicative.

Data sources

  • Calgary Transit — CTrain, MAX and bus network (GTFS)
  • Calgary Transit — GTFS-realtime vehicle positions, for the Live layer
  • Airdrie Transit — rebuilt from the City's route and stop GIS plus published timetables
  • Town of Cochrane — COLT, rebuilt from the Town's route and stop GIS plus published timetables
  • Falcon Bus — Cochrane and Okotoks commuter corridors, approximated from published timetables
  • Roam Transit, Banff and Canmore — approximated from published timetables

Calgary Transit's feed is used under the Open Government Licence – City of Calgary. The approximated operators publish no GTFS; their timetables are used as the source and the resulting routes are marked as approximations on the map.
