    How the ranking works

    Every listing is scored as pack size × volume × ABV ÷ price, then shown as millilitres of pure alcohol per dollar. Sale prices are used when they are lower; deposit and HST are excluded.

    Data comes from The Beer Store's public store and product listings, proxied through a Cloudflare Worker. Prices change; check the shelf tag.
