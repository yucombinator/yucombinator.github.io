Ottawa in Motion

Every scheduled trip across Ottawa and Gatineau — O-Train Lines 1, 2 and 4, the OC Transpo bus network, STO buses and the Rapibus — drawn as arrows moving along their routes. Switch between the latest weekday, Saturday and Sunday, and click any arrow or line for that route's stops and stats.

This is mostly the timetable. Each arrow is interpolated along its GTFS shape between its first and last stop time; delays and cancellations are not included. The bar chart counts scheduled departures per 10 minutes for the modes you enable.

OC Transpo and STO both publish GTFS. The current STO export omits stop times, so this build uses the latest complete STO snapshot for Gatineau timetables; OC Transpo data is the current schedule. Click any route to see its timetable and summary.

The Future toggle shows the next phase of the network: Line 1 East from Blair to Trim, the Line 1 branch from Lincoln Fields to Algonquin, Line 3 west from Tunney's Pasture to Moodie, and the planned Gatineau–Ottawa tram. The O-Train lines use the completed track alignments from the City's Stage 2 dataset; headways and the tram alignment are indicative.

Data: OC Transpo and STO GTFS feeds, used under their open-data licences. O-Train lines use their line colours; OC Transpo buses, STO buses and Rapibus use operator colours. . Basemap © OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap.

Open source: the code behind this series lives in the visualizations folder on GitHub.

Inspired by Montreal in Motion by Allemanns Data. Built by Yu Chen Hou.

Data sources

  • OC Transpo — O-Train, buses and stops (GTFS)
  • STO — Gatineau buses and Rapibus (GTFS; latest complete snapshot)
  • OpenStreetMap — dedicated busways and bus lanes (Overpass)
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap — basemap

OC Transpo data is used under the City of Ottawa Open Data Licence. STO data is used under the STO open-data terms. The current STO GTFS export has an empty stop_times.txt; the latest complete snapshot is used until the operator's export is repaired.
