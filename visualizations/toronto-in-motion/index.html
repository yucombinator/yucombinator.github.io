Toronto in Motion

Every scheduled trip across the Greater Golden Horseshoe and Waterloo Region — TTC, GO Transit, UP Express, MiWay, Brampton, YRT, Durham, Oakville, Burlington, HSR, GRT/ION, Guelph, Barrie, Milton, Simcoe County LINX, Orangeville, Lindsay and Niagara Region — animated as a dot moving along its route, with a 24-hour scrubber. Switch between the most recent weekday, Saturday and Sunday to see how service thins out on weekends.

Live mode (the red button) shows actual vehicle positions from the agencies that publish open GTFS-Realtime: TTC surface, Brampton, YRT, Durham, HSR, GRT/ION and Guelph. Those feeds block direct browser requests, so a scheduled GitHub Actions job decodes them and publishes a snapshot to the site; the page picks it up, jumps the clock to the snapshot time, and routes without realtime keep their scheduled positions at that time. The button only appears while the snapshot is less than 30 minutes old.

Outside live mode this is the timetable, not live tracking. Each dot is a scheduled trip interpolated along its official GTFS shape between its first and last stop time, at uniform speed. No vehicle positions, delays or cancellations are included.

Data: the agencies' published GTFS feeds, each used under its open data licence (TTC, Metrolinx, MiWay, Brampton, YRT, Durham, Oakville, Burlington, Hamilton, Region of Waterloo, Guelph, Barrie, Milton, Simcoe County, Orangeville, Kawartha Lakes, Niagara Region). TTC lines use the TTC's own colours — Line 1 yellow, Line 2 green, Line 4 magenta, Line 5 orange, Line 6 grey, streetcars red. Other agencies are coloured for legibility, not to match their brands.

Basemap: OpenFreeMap, built from OpenStreetMap data. You can drag and zoom the map; the dots are reprojected to follow it.

Format and interaction inspired by Montreal in Motion by Allemanns Data.
