The US immigration system, as a queue

One person, no legal status, every path out. Each step names the form, the agency that processes it, and roughly how long the wait is.

About these numbers

USCIS publishes the time within which it completed 80% of cases for each form. That is not a median and not a promise. About 4 months means 80% finished inside 4 months.

Figures are rounded and compiled September 2026 from public USCIS and Department of Labor processing times. They do not update themselves.
