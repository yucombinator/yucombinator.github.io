Visualizations

Things I have made to make a pile of data legible: transit systems in motion, maps, and explainers.

In Motion

City-scale transit animations — every scheduled trip, animated across a 24-hour clock, with live vehicle overlays where open data makes it possible.

3 cities

Toronto in Motion

Every scheduled trip across the Greater Toronto Area and Waterloo Region — 79,370 of them, from TTC and GO to a dozen local agencies — animated as moving dots, with a 24-hour scrubber.

Vancouver in Motion

Every scheduled trip across Metro Vancouver, the Fraser Valley and the Sea-to-Sky — SkyTrain, buses, SeaBus, BC Ferries, Aquabus and Hullo — animated as moving dots, with a 24-hour scrubber.

San Francisco in Motion

Every scheduled trip across the Bay Area — Muni, BART, Caltrain, AC Transit, VTA, ferries and more — animated with a 24-hour scrubber.

Seattle in Motion

Every scheduled trip across Puget Sound — Link, Sounder, Seattle's streetcars, King County Metro and the regional buses, Washington State Ferries and the Monorail — with a live vehicle layer.

Maps & explainers

Interactive maps and diagrams for questions that are easier to see than to read.

WA Power Rates

An interactive map of Washington electric utility service territories, shaded by what a residential customer pays.

National Park Checklist

All 111 US and Canadian national parks on one map, with gateway towns, airports, drive times and entry rules. Tick off the ones you have seen and star the ones you want to visit.

Immigration Queues

A flowchart of the ways out for someone living in the US without legal status, showing each required form and how long it takes.
