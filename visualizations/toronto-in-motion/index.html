Toronto in Motion

Every scheduled TTC trip for a regular Wednesday — 40,001 of them — animated as a dot moving along its route. Subways, streetcars and light rail, and buses, with a 24-hour scrubber.

This is the timetable, not live tracking. Each dot is a scheduled trip interpolated along its official GTFS shape between its first and last stop time, at uniform speed. No vehicle positions, delays or cancellations are included.

Data: TTC Routes and Schedules (GTFS, October 2026 extract), used under the City of Toronto Open Data Licence. Line 5 Eglinton and Line 6 Finch West are grouped with streetcars because that is how they are coded in the feed.

Basemap: OpenFreeMap, built from OpenStreetMap data. You can drag and zoom the map; the dots are reprojected to follow it.
