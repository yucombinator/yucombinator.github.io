Seattle in Motion

Every scheduled trip across Puget Sound — Link, Sounder, the Seattle streetcars, King County Metro and the regional bus systems, Washington State Ferries, the Kitsap fast ferries and the Seattle Center Monorail — drawn as arrows moving along their routes. Switch between the latest weekday, Saturday and Sunday, and click any arrow or line for that route's stops and stats.

This is the timetable, not live tracking. Each arrow is interpolated along its GTFS shape between its first and last stop time; delays and cancellations are not included. The bar chart counts scheduled departures per 10 minutes for the modes you enable.

Data: the Puget Sound consolidated GTFS feed, published by the region's agencies under their open data licences. . Link, the streetcars and Sounder use their line colours; regional buses and ferries are coloured by operator for legibility. BRT lines (RapidRide, Swift, Stride, Stream) are red; dedicated busways and painted bus lanes from OpenStreetMap are outlined on the map. Basemap © OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap.

Open source: the code behind this series lives in the visualizations folder on GitHub.

Inspired by Montreal in Motion by Allemanns Data. Built by Yu Chen Hou.
