San Francisco in Motion

Every scheduled trip across the Bay Area and Santa Cruz coast — Muni, BART (including eBART), Caltrain, AC Transit, VTA, SamTrans, Santa Cruz METRO, Golden Gate Transit, SMART, county buses, bridge shuttles and the bay ferries — drawn as arrows moving along their routes. Switch between the latest weekday, Saturday and Sunday, and click any arrow or line for that route's stops and stats.

Use the Future toggle in the legend to show planned and under-construction extensions as dashed ghost lines with approximate headways.

This is mostly the timetable. Each arrow is interpolated along its GTFS shape between its first and last stop time; delays and cancellations are not included. The bar chart counts scheduled departures per 10 minutes for the modes you enable.

Data: GTFS feeds from Bay Area transit agencies, used under their open data licences. . Rail lines use their published colours; buses are coloured by operator; ferries use distinct colours. Basemap © OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap.

Open source: the code behind this series lives in the visualizations folder on GitHub.

Inspired by Montreal in Motion by Allemanns Data. Built by Yu Chen Hou.
