Every territory is shaded by how its price compares with your county's utility.

All-hours colors the standard residential rate. This hour uses time-of-day schedules where published — for Puget Sound Energy, City Light, Avista and Pacific Power those are opt-in products, not what most customers actually pay.

Territories are approximate: census county boundaries with a nearest-service-center model, not legal service areas. Rates are curated from each utility's published tariff and do not update on their own — see the README for sources and effective dates. Non-investor-owned rates are set by locally elected utility boards, not the WUTC.
