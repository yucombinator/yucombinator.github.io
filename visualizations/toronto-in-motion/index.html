Toronto in Motion

Every scheduled trip across the Greater Golden Horseshoe — TTC, GO, UP Express, 15 local agencies and the Island ferry — drawn as arrows moving along their routes. Switch between the latest weekday, Saturday and Sunday, and click any arrow or line for that route's stops and stats.

This is the timetable, not live tracking. Each arrow is interpolated along its GTFS shape between its first and last stop time; delays and cancellations are not included. The bar chart counts scheduled departures per 10 minutes for the modes you enable.

Data: the agencies' published GTFS feeds under their open data licences; the Island ferry uses the City's winter timetable. TTC lines use TTC colours; other agencies are coloured for legibility. Basemap © OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap.
